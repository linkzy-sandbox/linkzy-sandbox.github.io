title: "Showing customer reviews on your website"
---

Reviews do not have to stay on the sites where customers leave them. Quoting a few on the relevant service pages, with the customer's permission, puts reassurance exactly where a visitor is deciding whether to get in touch.

A review is most convincing when it is specific. One sentence about a named job in a named area tells a visitor more than a row of stars, so it is worth choosing reviews that describe what was actually done.

Keeping the selection current matters. A page that shows reviews from several years ago suggests nothing has happened since, while a recent one shows the business is active and still earning good feedback.
