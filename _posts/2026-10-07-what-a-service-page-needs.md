title: "What every service page needs"
---

A service page has one job, which is to help a visitor decide whether this business can solve their problem. It should say plainly what the service is, who it is for, where it is offered and how to get in touch.

Visitors skim before they read, so the most important facts belong near the top. A clear heading, a short summary and an obvious way to make contact do more for the page than several paragraphs of background.

Proof matters as much as description. Photographs of finished work, a few words from past customers and straightforward answers to common questions give a visitor reasons to believe what the page says.
