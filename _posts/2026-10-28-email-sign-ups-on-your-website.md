title: "Collecting email sign-ups on your website"
---

An email sign-up form gives visitors who are not ready to buy a way to stay in touch. It works best when it says exactly what subscribers will receive and how often, so nobody is surprised by the first message.

Placement matters more than design. A short form at the end of a helpful article reaches people at the moment they have just found the content useful, which is when they are most willing to hear more.

What happens after sign-up decides whether the list is worth having. A prompt welcome email and a steady, modest schedule of useful messages keep subscribers reading instead of reaching for the unsubscribe link.
