title: "Reading your website's search data"
---

Search data shows which words people typed before they arrived at a website and which pages they landed on. Looking at it once a month is enough for most small businesses to notice what is working and what is being ignored.

The most useful view is the list of pages that appear often in search but are rarely clicked. A clearer page title and description is usually the fix, because those two lines are all a searcher sees before deciding.

Local phrases deserve particular attention. Searches that include a town or neighbourhood name show where the website is already visible in local search and where a dedicated page for an area might be worth writing.
